What does HedSpace design and manufacture?

Specialised solutions for satellite transport, storage and handling. That covers controlled-climate satellite transfer containers, satellite interface disks, and the mounting structures, plates and adapters that attach a satellite securely to the equipment.

Can a container be built to custom dimensions?

Yes. There are two standard formats — Large at 3200 × 2100 × 2058 mm and Small at 1720 × 1720 × 2055 mm (FRAME S) — and custom sizes and configurations are available on request, based on the satellite, the transport conditions and the operational requirements.

Do you sell containers, lease them, or both?

Both. HedSpace offers satellite transport containers for sale and for lease, with configurations tailored to the needs of each mission.

How is the payload protected inside FRAME S?

FRAME S is rated IP66: dust-tight and protected against powerful water jets. It is drop-test certified and vibration tested, it carries pressure-relief valves for air transport, and the payload sits on a circular mounting plate on an isolated platform, behind EPDM sealing gaskets.

How do you reach the satellite inside FRAME S?

From the side. Two cover sections open left and right, so you do not need a high roof or overhead clearance. Each cover section weighs 60 kg and is easy to remove, disassembly is tool-free, and no torque wrench is required.

Do you support the equipment after delivery?

Yes. HedSpace provides inspection, maintenance and repair, as well as modifications and upgrades. Technical support continues beyond delivery.
